## Teacher Q&A Cheat Sheet (Also accessible on screen with Key `Q`)

### 1. Why Italy? Why Siena and not Milan or Rome?
- **English**: Italy combines strong Computer Science and AI programs with rich culture and 0 EUR host tuition under Erasmus+. I chose the University of Siena over Milan and Rome because it is already a confirmed IT partner on the EKA partner list, teaches its AI Master’s fully in English, and costs approximately 600–950 EUR per month instead of 850–1,300 EUR.
- **O‘zbekcha**: Italiya kuchli Kompyuter fanlari va sun’iy intellekt dasturlarini boy madaniyat hamda Erasmus+ doirasida 0 yevro kontrakt bilan birlashtiradi. Men Milan va Rim o‘rniga Siena universitetini tanladim, chunki u EKA hamkorlar ro‘yxatida IT bo‘yicha tasdiqlangan, AI magistraturasini to‘liq ingliz tilida o‘qitadi va oylik xarajati 850–1300 yevro o‘rniga taxminan 600–950 yevroni tashkil qiladi.

### 2. How much does it cost and does the Erasmus+ grant cover it?
- **English**: Monthly living costs are approximately 600–950 EUR in Siena, 850–1,200 EUR in Rome, and 900–1,300 EUR in Milan, while tuition at the host university is 0 EUR. The Erasmus+ mobility grant helps cover a large part of rent and food, and for the exact monthly grant rate for Italy this year I would check with the EKA Erasmus+ coordinator.
- **O‘zbekcha**: Oylik yashash xarajatlari Sienada taxminan 600–950 yevro, Rimda 850–1200 yevro va Milanda 900–1300 yevroni tashkil etadi, qabul qiluvchi universitetda o‘qish esa 0 yevro. Erasmus+ stipendiyasi ijara va oziq-ovqatning katta qismini qoplashga yordam beradi, Italiya uchun bu yilgi aniq oylik stipendiya miqdorini esa EKA Erasmus+ koordinatoridan aniqlashtirgan bo‘lardim.

### 3. What language do I need? What level?
- **English**: For the University of Siena’s MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Automation Engineering, courses are taught in English, and Milano-Bicocca requires at least B2 English. For daily life in Italy or Italian-taught courses at Roma Tre, B1 Italian is recommended.
- **O‘zbekcha**: Siena universitetining Sun’iy intellekt va avtomatlashtirish magistratura dasturida darslar ingliz tilida o‘tiladi, Milano-Bikokka esa kamida B2 darajadagi ingliz tilini talab qiladi. Italiyadagi kundalik hayot yoki Roma Tredagi italyan tilidagi fanlar uchun B1 darajadagi italyan tili tavsiya etiladi.

### 4. What is ECTS and how are credits recognized at EKA?
- **English**: ECTS is the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System, and in Italy 1 CFU credit equals 1 ECTS credit, with a full semester equal to 30 ECTS. Before departure, you sign an Erasmus+ Learning Agreement so the credits you pass in Italy are recognized toward your EKA degree.
- **O‘zbekcha**: ECTS — Yevropa kreditlarni ko‘chirish va jamg‘arish tizimi bo‘lib, Italiyada 1 CFU krediti 1 ECTS kreditiga, to‘liq bir semestr esa 30 ECTS ga teng. Jo‘nab ketishdan oldin Erasmus+ O‘quv shartnomasi (Learning Agreement) imzolanadi va Italiyada topshirgan kreditlaringiz EKA diplomiga hisobga olinadi.

### 5. How do I apply and when are the deadlines?
- **English**: At EKA, you need a minimum average grade of 7.0 and no study debts to apply for Erasmus+ study mobility. Applications open in October for the spring semester and in March for the autumn semester through the EKA Erasmus+ office.
- **O‘zbekcha**: EKAda Erasmus+ o‘quv almashinuviga ariza topshirish uchun o‘rtacha baho kamida 7.0 bo‘lishi va akademik qarzdorlik bo‘lmasligi kerak. Arizalar bahorgi semestr uchun oktabr oyida, kuzgi semestr uchun esa mart oyida EKA Erasmus+ bo‘limi orqali qabul qilinadi.

### 6. What about health insurance abroad? (EHIC + insurance)
- **English**: As students from an EU country, we must bring a free European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) for public medical care in Italy. In addition, EKA and the Italian host universities recommend private travel and accident insurance for full coverage.
- **O‘zbekcha**: Yevropa Ittifoqi davlati talabalari sifatida biz Italiyada davlat tibbiy yordamidan foydalanish uchun bepul Yevropa tibbiy sug‘urta kartasini (EHIC) olib borishimiz shart. Bundan tashqari, to‘liq himoya uchun EKA va Italiya universitetlari xususiy sayohat hamda baxtsiz hodisalardan sug‘urtani ham tavsiya qiladi.

### 7. What if I do not speak Italian?
- **English**: You can still study in Italy by choosing the University of Siena or English-taught modules at Milano-Bicocca, where classes and exams are in English. Host universities also offer introductory Italian language courses for Erasmus+ students to help with everyday life.
- **O‘zbekcha**: Agar italyan tilini bilmasangiz ham, dars va imtihonlari ingliz tilida bo‘lgan Siena universitetini yoki Milano-Bikokkadagi inglizcha modullarni tanlab o‘qishingiz mumkin. Shuningdek, qabul qiluvchi universitetlar kundalik hayotda yordam berish uchun Erasmus+ talabalariga boshlang‘ich italyan tili kurslarini taklif etadi.
